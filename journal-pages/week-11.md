# Week 11

## Self Review

Three key moments in my projects are probably the following; collecting data and understanding the inner workings of p5.js, figuring out what programs/"mediums" to use for my project, and finding methods to make my project visually appealing.

Collecting the data became less of a concern as I make a reasonable pivot (explained below) which also enhances my project futher. That aside, it was also very interesting to see just how much you could do with p5.js. Variables were incredibly useful for me, as that was how I was able to insert data through APIs, and also script some interesting ways to make my project have visual interest. 

Figuring out exactly what mediums I wanted to use in order to demonstrate my project was also important; I tried using various types of Figma formats through trial and error, and ended up utilising Figma Sites (even though my project is, indeed, not a site). It had the capabilities of mimicking the workings of a phone while also having the exact tools that I required to make my hand in look tidy.

Lastly, I was incredibly happy with figuring out different methods to make my hand in look good. Utilising the sine wave an its oscillation was incredibly smart, and the pay off of understanding the script was incredibly satisfying.
