## Drafting with AI 

Using my Making Journal and the data source, I did the AI draftign activity. Here are the results.

I am a designer exploring the integration of technologically intricate skills into the field of data-driven communication
. This visualisation is the result of a project developed for "Designing with Data," where I have transformed raw statistical information into a visual narrative for public engagement
. The work is built upon data from the Ministry of Business, Innovation, and Employment, sourced through Figure.NZ, which tracks the weekly adjusted retail prices for Premium petrol, Regular petrol, and Diesel across New Zealand
.
The subject matter focuses on a period of intense volatility in early 2026, where international developments in the Middle East led to dramatic price increases—most notably seen in diesel prices, which rose from approximately $1.87 to over $3.80 per litre in just two months
. This data is framed within a conceptual timeline that moves from a "Doomsday" proposal to a "Freedom" showcase, reflecting the precarious and shifting nature of the economic landscape
.
Critically, this work examines the transition from "unprofessional" or casual data representation toward a technologically sophisticated and professionally presented format
. By moving beyond a random assortment of images and "normal people writing," I seek to bridge the gap between amateur interest in computer science and the professional rigor required for design
. The intended impact is to provide the public with a clear, direct encounter with the complex factors that influence their daily costs, such as the Emissions Trading Scheme, regional taxes, and importer margins
. My goal is to use technical intricacy to ensure this vital information remains accessible and impactful, helping the audience navigate the "Doomsday" of fluctuating prices toward a sense of "Freedom" through informed perspective
